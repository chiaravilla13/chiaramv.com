About

Strategy before aesthetics. Clarity before production.

I’m Chiara M. Villa, a Brand & Communication Strategist. I help professionals, consultants and service brands define their positioning and translate it into identity, messaging and communication materials.

I connect research with practical delivery: understanding what makes a business relevant and expressing it through the words and materials it uses to present itself.

What guides my work

Understanding

We clarify goals, audience and value. A strong brand is readable because it knows what it stands for, who it serves and why it should be chosen.

Context

Method turns context into decisions: analysis, direction, system. A brand should not follow the mood of the moment. It should follow a trajectory.

Continuity

Identity and messaging should speak the same language across website, social channels and touchpoints, even as the project grows.

Background

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Public Relations and a master’s degree in Marketing, with a focus on Brand Management. My further training covers AI for marketing, semiotics and writing.

I spent four years working in corporate communication, covering newsletters, website copy, internal communication, brand materials and events. This experience showed me the importance of shared criteria when different people and channels represent the same organisation.

Today, I combine strategic analysis and writing with practical skills in content structure, SEO and HTML/CSS. I use AI tools where useful, reviewing their outputs and taking responsibility for decisions and deliverables.

  • Decision-oriented approach
  • Focus on applicability: a system that holds over time
  • Attention to UX, SEO and microcopy

How I work

I start with your business: goals, offer, audience and practical challenges. I analyse the context and develop recommendations with a clear rationale, which we discuss together before applying them to identity, copy and materials.
