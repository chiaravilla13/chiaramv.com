Services

Direction, messaging, system.

I help you define your positioning, build a recognisable identity and translate it into communication. We can work on a complete programme or a specific need, starting with your priorities and reviewing the strategic foundations already in place.

Let’s talk about your project

A clear scope before we begin

Each proposal specifies activities, deliverables, revisions, timing and fees. It distinguishes strategic work from the production of materials and identifies any external costs, such as printing, photography or technical development.

Brand direction

We clarify context, alternatives, offer and perception to understand what should guide the brand: positioning, value proposition, differentiators and key messages.

What it includes

  • Context and alternatives analysis
  • Reading the offer, perception and friction points
  • Value proposition and direction
  • Key messages and proof points
  • Strategic priorities: what should lead first

What you receive

A strategy document bringing together the research findings, priority audience, positioning, value proposition and key messages, with the rationale behind the choices and priorities for putting them into practice.

Ideal for

Professionals and businesses launching an activity, rethinking their offer or looking for a more precise way to explain why customers should choose them.

Messaging and brand system

From direction to system: identity, tone, principles and guidelines to make the brand speak with greater coherence, continuity and recognisability.

What it includes

  • Brand values, promise and principles
  • Personality and tone of voice
  • Visual direction and coherence criteria
  • Usage rules and continuity guidelines

What you receive

A brand manual covering personality, promise, tone of voice, writing examples and visual principles. A guide you can use directly and share with the people creating your copy, design and content.

Any logo design or revision, templates and individual materials are specified in the proposal, together with the agreed deliverables.

Ideal for

Those who want to stop communicating in fragments and build a more coherent presence across website, social channels, materials and collaborators.

Touchpoint clarity

We make what already exists — or what you are building — easier to read across website, LinkedIn, materials and content: hierarchy, messaging, tone, microcopy and priorities.

What it includes

  • Core message and supporting messages
  • Tone of voice and readability criteria
  • Review of touchpoints and friction points
  • Guidelines for content and applications

What you receive

A review of the agreed touchpoints, identifying problems, priorities and recommended changes. Depending on the scope, this can include rewritten copy, page structures, content guidelines and an action plan.

Ideal for

Businesses whose website, social profiles or materials present them inconsistently, or those preparing new communications and looking to coordinate them.

Focused support

We can also work on a specific need. Before defining the scope, we review your goals, available materials and existing strategic decisions.

Website structure and content

I organise pages, information and navigation paths to help visitors understand your offer and find what they need.

  • Page map and content hierarchy
  • Wireframes for the agreed pages
  • Recommendations for messaging, buttons and microcopy

Final visual design and technical development are assessed and quoted separately.

Copy and materials review

I examine the agreed pages and materials to identify unclear messages, inconsistencies and missing information.

  • Assessment of structure, tone and messaging
  • Identified problems and annotated examples
  • Recommendations ordered by priority

Full copy rewrites can be included in the proposal if required.

Implementation support

I help you translate strategy into consistent copy, content and materials, working on the agreed applications.

  • Writing or reviewing the specified copy
  • Templates and materials defined in the scope
  • Checks for consistency with the brand guidelines

Activities, quantities and frequency of support are agreed before work begins.
