Results

What changes when the brand has direction.

Brand work helps clarify your offer, coordinate identity and messaging, and establish criteria for communication. Here you’ll find the changes we work towards and some illustrative examples. Goals and deliverables are defined for each project.

The goals of a complete programme

More clarity

Goals, audience and differentiators become easier to read: the brand knows what to say and what to avoid.

More consistency

Messaging, visuals and tone follow the same direction: less dispersion, more identity.

More recognisability

A replicable system: guidelines and materials reduce improvisation and inconsistent interpretation.

Before / After

These examples describe starting situations and project goals. They are illustrative, rather than comparisons from documented projects.

From confusing to readable

Situation: “We do many things, and it is hard to explain them.”

Goal: a clear proposition, supported by messages people can remember.

From variable to recognisable

Before- tone and visuals change depending on who is publishing.

After- a system that guides: consistency across website, social and materials.

From taste-based to criteria-based

Before- every choice becomes an endless discussion.

After- clear rules: decisions become faster and better.

How we assess the work

At the outset, we agree on what we want to improve and what we will assess. We distinguish the quality of the deliverables from the effects of putting them into practice.

Clarity of the offer

We check that the materials explain what you offer, who it is for and what supports your value proposition.

Consistency across applications

We review copy and materials against the guidelines: messaging, tone and visual elements should support the agreed strategic choices.

Feedback after implementation

If monitoring is included in the project, we review available data and collected feedback. Metrics, timeframes and measurement methods are agreed according to the goals.

Enquiries, sales and retention also depend on the offer, pricing, distribution and execution. Any changes need to be interpreted in context.
